# Smart Local

Smart Local is a local digital marketing and AI search agency based in Glasshouse Mountains, Queensland. We help local businesses from the Sunshine Coast to Brisbane get found in Google and in AI search tools.

**Website:** [smart-local.com.au](https://smart-local.com.au/)

## What we work on
- Local SEO and AI search (GEO / AEO) visibility
- Google Ads for local service businesses
- Fast, crawlable websites for local businesses

## Public projects
- [ai-search-readiness](https://github.com/smart-local/ai-search-readiness): command-line checker for Google and AI search readiness (schema, NAP consistency, AI crawler access in robots.txt, llms.txt)
- [local-business-schema](https://github.com/smart-local/local-business-schema): JSON-LD templates and notes for local business structured data
